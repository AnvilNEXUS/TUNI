✨ Band 7.5+ Model Answer

A remarkable place I visited that offers incredible views of marine wildlife is The Sea Shell Aquarium, located within the VinWonders theme park on Phu Quoc Island. The facility itself is an architectural marvel, housed inside a massive building shaped like a giant hawksbill turtle situated right along the coast.

What makes this aquarium truly spectacular is its state-of-the-art underwater tunnel, which provides visitors with an immersive, 360-degree panorama of ocean life. As you walk through the glass enclosure, you are completely surrounded by thousands of fascinating marine creatures. I was fortunate enough to observe a vast array of species, ranging from majestic sharks and playful penguins to colorful clownfish, blue tangs, delicate seahorses, and vibrant coral reef ecosystems.

While marveling at these animals, I couldn't help but reflect on the severe threats facing marine life today. In recent years, issues such as industrial waste dumping, oil spills, and overfishing have severely disrupted oceanic habitats. Furthermore, international controversies surrounding commercial whaling in countries like Japan highlight how vulnerable large sea mammals are to human exploitation.

Seeing these creatures up close was a deeply eye-opening experience. It reinforced for me how crucial it is to enforce international marine protections, combat pollution, and preserve our oceans for future generations.
