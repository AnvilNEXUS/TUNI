Section 08: Spelling Rules & Adverb Formation (Questions 46–50)

Transform adjectives to adverbs and eliminate frequent orthographical mistakes.

Spelling Reference:
• Regular adjectives: add -ly (e.g. rapidly)
• Ending in -l: double l (e.g. carefully)
• Ending in consonant + -y: -ily (e.g. steadily)
• Ending in -le: drop -e, add -y (e.g. gently)
• Ending in -ic: add -ally (e.g. dramatically)
