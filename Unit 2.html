✨ Band 7.5+ Model Answer

I’d like to talk about my ultimate role model, Cristiano Ronaldo, who is widely considered one of the greatest football players in history. His extraordinary athletic achievements include winning five Ballon d'Or awards, five UEFA Champions League titles, and numerous domestic league trophies across Europe.

Born in 1985 on the island of Madeira, Portugal, Ronaldo grew up in a financially disadvantaged household. At the tender age of 12, he made the difficult decision to leave his family behind and move to Lisbon to join Sporting CP’s youth academy. This transition was far from smooth—he experienced severe homesickness and faced relentless teasing from peers due to his regional accent. To make matters worse, at age 15, he was diagnosed with a racing heart condition that threatened to end his career before it even began. Fortunately, he underwent a successful laser heart operation and returned to the pitch fully recovered.

Another immense hardship occurred in 2005 when he tragically lost his father to alcohol-related illness. Despite this devastating personal tragedy, Ronaldo channeled his grief into an unmatched work ethic. His career trajectory skyrocketed after joining Manchester United under the mentorship of Sir Alex Ferguson, who acted as a father figure and helped refine his talent into world-class performance.

What I find most inspiring about Ronaldo is his unyielding determination and resilience. He proved that raw talent is meaningless without continuous self-discipline. His journey has inspired millions to pursue their ambitions—including modern football stars like Kylian Mbappé and Erling Haaland. On a personal level, his 'never-give-up' mindset serves as a constant source of motivation whenever I encounter daunting challenges in my studies or daily life.
