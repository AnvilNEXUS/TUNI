✨ Band 7.5+ Model Answer

I’d like to share a memorable journey I took about five months ago from Ho Chi Minh City to Can Tho in the Mekong Delta. I had just wrapped up a stressful series of final exams at university, so I was desperate for a short getaway to unwind and recharge my batteries.

For this trip, I decided to travel by an overnight sleeper coach operated by Phuong Trang, one of Vietnam's most established long-distance bus companies. I booked an individual compartment berth, which was surprisingly cozy and equipped with a lie-flat bed, soft pillow, and privacy curtains, allowing passengers to catch up on sleep during the transit.

What made this journey particularly unforgettable was the mesmerizing landscape I woke up to. As the sun began to rise, I opened the window blinds and was greeted by breathtaking views of the southern countryside. Endless stretches of vibrant golden rice paddies, lush dragon fruit farms, and winding tributaries flooded the horizon. To my surprise, the bus arrived in Can Tho in just under seven hours—considerably faster than the ten-hour estimate noted on the schedule.

All in all, it turned out to be a wonderfully restorative experience. It made me appreciate that opting for overland public transport isn't just about reaching a destination, but also about enjoying the scenic route along the way.
